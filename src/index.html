Crescendo vocabulary

Starts with single words, grows into full grammar.

Core words

I, you, want, go, stop, help, more, all done, like, do not, can, where, and what.

Fringe words

Personal and specific vocabulary such as pizza, dinosaur, soccer, grandma, Minecraft, and doctor.

Progressive language

Beginner, intermediate, and advanced levels reveal more words as communication skills develop.

Color coding

Pronouns are yellow, verbs green, nouns orange, descriptors blue, and social words pink.

Voices and languages

Communication can sound like the person using it.

Samy supports child, teen, and adult voice choices across English, Arabic, French, and Dutch, including bilingual sentences when families use more than one language.

Access for more bodies and brains

Built for early communication, motor access, and literacy.

⇥ Switch access

Scanning support helps people select words without direct touch.

◐ VoiceOver

Clear labels and focus order support users with visual impairments.

▣ Large buttons

Adjustable layouts and bigger targets reduce motor effort.

⌗ Keyguards

Physical overlays and generous spacing help prevent accidental taps.
